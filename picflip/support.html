PicFlip Support

Turn HEIC and HEIF photos into JPEG copies, then save to Photos, save to Files, or share them.

Convert a photo

  1. Choose Select HEIC Photos or Import from Files.
  2. Select your photos and tap Convert to JPEG.
  3. Choose Save to Photos, Save to Files, or Share Files.

Original photos stay unchanged. The photo gallery shows only HEIC and HEIF images. JPEG, PNG and other formats cannot be imported.

Free allowance and Pro

You can convert 10 HEIC or HEIF photos per calendar month for free. The allowance resets on the first day of the month at 00:00 UTC. The app shows the reset date and the number of days remaining. Saving, sharing, and reopening completed JPEGs do not use another free photo.

Pro removes the monthly conversion limit. Available plans and local prices appear in the app. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled in Apple Account settings. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Free and Pro conversions both use full resolution and stay on your device.

Restore or manage a purchase

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen while signed into the Apple Account used to buy Pro. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple subscription settings. For refund requests, visit Apple Report a Problem.

Troubleshooting

No HEIC photos shown? Only HEIC/HEIF photos from your allowed library are shown. If you chose limited photo access, include HEIC photos in your permitted selection using iPhone Settings. You can also import a HEIC file from Files.

Cannot save to Photos? Allow PicFlip to add photos in iPhone Settings, or save to Files instead.

Photo cannot be imported? Make sure an iCloud photo or file is downloaded. PicFlip imports HEIC and HEIF images. Damaged files, unsupported image formats, and images larger than 100 megapixels cannot be processed.

Recent files disappeared? History is temporary: sessions are kept for up to seven days, and iOS may clear cached files sooner. Save completed JPEGs to Photos or Files to keep them.

Prices are unavailable? Check your internet connection and tap Reload prices. Your monthly free allowance still resets automatically.
