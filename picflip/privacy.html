PicFlip Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

PicFlip converts HEIC and HEIF photos to JPEG on your iPhone. The app has no account system, photo upload server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking.

Your photos stay under your control

PicFlip imports only the photos or files you select. Conversion happens on your device. Saving to Photos requests add-only access to create new JPEG copies. Your originals are not modified or deleted.

Saving to Files or sharing sends copies to the destination you choose. That destination may use cloud services, such as iCloud Drive, and its own privacy policy applies. Local conversion works without an internet connection; purchases, cloud file downloads, support pages, and some sharing destinations may require one.

Local storage and retention

Imported copies, generated JPEGs, and recent conversion history are stored in the app cache. PicFlip removes cached sessions older than seven days when launched. iOS can remove cached files sooner. Save files you want to keep to Photos or Files.

The monthly free conversion counter is stored locally in the iPhone Keychain and can remain after uninstalling the app. It is not sent to the developer. The allowance renews at 00:00 UTC on the first day of each calendar month; reset dates are shown in your local time. App preferences, including introduction completion, are stored locally.

Photo metadata

HEIC to JPEG conversion preserves image dimensions, applies orientation, and removes location metadata. A capture date may be preserved. Existing JPEG files are copied unchanged and may retain their existing metadata. Check what you share if a photo contains sensitive information.

Purchases

Apple processes and manages in-app purchases. PicFlip reads Apple-verified purchase information on your device to determine whether Pro is active. We do not receive your payment card details or operate a purchase database. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's services.

Contact and this website

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive photos unless needed for your issue. Ask us to delete a support conversation when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable retention obligations.

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to serve and protect this website. This website contains no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

You can manage photo permissions in iPhone Settings, remove saved exports from their chosen destinations, and uninstall PicFlip to remove its app storage. The local Keychain allowance may persist after uninstalling. There is no PicFlip account or server-side photo library to delete. For privacy requests or applicable access, correction, and deletion rights relating to support correspondence, contact fridgerio.app@gmail.com.

We may update this policy when the app's practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
